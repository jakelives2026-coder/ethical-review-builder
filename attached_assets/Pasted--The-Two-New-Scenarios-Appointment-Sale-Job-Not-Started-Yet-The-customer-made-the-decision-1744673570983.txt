✅ Appointment → Sale → Job Not Started Yet

The customer made the decision and purchased, but installation/service hasn’t begun

We want to capture their confidence in the process, rep, and setup

✅ Appointment → Sale → Job Completed

This is the final full-service review, routed to the existing Customer Flow
