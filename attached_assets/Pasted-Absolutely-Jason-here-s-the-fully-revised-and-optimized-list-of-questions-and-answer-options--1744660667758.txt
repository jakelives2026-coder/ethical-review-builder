## ✅ Acquaintance Review Flow – Questions & Pre-Filled Answer Options

---

### **Q1: How do you know or follow this business?**  
_(Goal: Establish familiarity or connection without claiming to be a customer)_

**Prompt shown to user:**  
*“How do you know about this business or the people behind it?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “I know someone on their team and have seen their professionalism firsthand.”
- “I follow their work on social media and see what they do regularly.”
- “I’ve referred others to them and heard great feedback.”
- “We’ve connected at local events or through mutual contacts.”
- “They’re known in the community and I often hear positive things.”

---

### **Q2: What makes them stand out in your opinion?**  
_(Goal: Reflect genuine, observable strengths that support a 5-star tone)_

**Prompt shown to user:**  
*“What stands out to you about this business or team?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “They come across as honest and truly care about doing great work.”
- “They consistently show up with professionalism and high standards.”
- “Their reputation and online presence make them easy to trust.”
- “They take the time to educate and support their audience.”
- “They’re active in the community and always represent themselves well.”

---

### **Q3: Would you feel confident recommending them to others?**  
_(Goal: Allow the user to express trust without overpromising)_

**Prompt shown to user:**  
*“Would you feel comfortable recommending them?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “Yes, I already have.”
- “Definitely — they’ve earned my confidence.”
- “Absolutely — I’ve seen enough to feel great recommending them.”
- “Yes — their reputation and presence speak for themselves.”
- “Without a doubt.”

---

### 🔄 Optional Personalization Screen (follows Q3)

**Prompt:**  
*“Want to personalize your review?”*

**Input:**  
- Text field: `Your first name (optional)`
