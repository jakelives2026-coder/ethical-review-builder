## ✅ Universal Customer Review Flow – Questions & Answer Options (Non-Industry Specific)

---

### **Q1: What service or product did you receive?**  
*(Goal: Ground the review in a real customer experience without naming a specific industry)*

**Prompt:**  
*“What did they help you with?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “They provided a helpful service I needed.”  
- “They delivered a product I had been looking for.”  
- “They guided me through a process I wasn’t familiar with.”  
- “They helped solve a problem I was dealing with.”  
- “They provided support or advice that made things easier.”  

---

### **Q2: What stood out to you about the experience?**  
*(Goal: Pull out trustworthy, review-worthy qualities that apply across industries)*

**Prompt:**  
*“What impressed you most about the experience?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “They were kind, professional, and easy to work with.”  
- “They explained everything clearly and answered all my questions.”  
- “The process was simple and smooth from start to finish.”  
- “They followed through on what they promised.”  
- “I felt taken care of and respected the entire time.”  

---

### **Q3: Would you recommend them to others?**  
*(Goal: Final signal of trust + confidence for the review reader)*

**Prompt:**  
*“Would you recommend them to someone you know?”*

**Answer options (single select):**
- “Yes, I already have.”  
- “Definitely — they earned my trust.”  
- “Yes — I’d gladly send friends or family their way.”  
- “Without hesitation.”  
- “Absolutely — they did a great job.”  

---

### ✨ Optional Personalization Screen

**Prompt:**  
*“Want to add your name to the review?”*

**Input Field:**  
- `Your first name (optional)`

**Helper Text:**  
“This is optional, but can help make your review feel more personal.”

---

This format ensures:
✅ Universality across all businesses  
✅ Consistent structure for AI to work with  
✅ Great UX with low effort from the user  
✅ Ethical and policy-safe review outputs
